## What still needs checking

- Launch the unsigned x64 application on the church's Windows computer.
- Decode and analyze the actual CD-ripped files and one normal song used by the church.
- Compare suggested boundaries and labels against listening-based markers.
- Check the real vocal/instrumental pair, including differences in intros, endings, and repeated verses.
- Audition jumps and repeated joins through the actual mixer/interface at rehearsal volume.
- Confirm save, restart, export, and restore on that Windows installation.

The section finder is a local musical-feature heuristic. Optional online preparation adds fingerprint recognition and duration-matched timed lyric clues. It does not transcribe the recording, remove vocals, or use a trained semantic music-structure model. It can give generic or incorrect section names, which need manual correction. The application's review workflow is part of preparing a usable song. Timed lyric stanza starts are approximate; repeated text does not guarantee a chorus, and similar recording durations do not guarantee identical arrangements.
